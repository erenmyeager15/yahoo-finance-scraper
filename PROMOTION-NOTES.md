## 60-Second Tutorial Script

1. Open the Actor and keep `AAPL` as the first symbol.
2. Run with `includeHistorical` off for the cheapest quote test.
3. Open the dataset and show price, previous close, change, volume, exchange, and market time.
4. Toggle historical prices for a small ticker set and show `historicalCount`.
5. Export to CSV/Excel or connect through the Apify API.
